Everything a collection team needs

One app from the field to head office.

Built the way collections are really done — quick at the borrower’s door, strict about the money, and clear months later when someone asks what happened to an account.

₹

Collect and issue receipts

Cash, UPI, cheque or bank transfer, with a numbered, GPS-stamped receipt the borrower gets on WhatsApp. Payments are allocated to the oldest instalment first.

⇅

A visit plan that ranks itself

Promises due today, broken promises, follow-ups and the most overdue loans come first — or sort by who is nearest. Log every visit and promise to pay.

🏦

Bank deposits, verified

When a borrower pays at the bank, the officer photographs the pay-in slip. A supervisor checks it against the bank before it counts, and a slip can never be used twice.

📶

Works without signal

Everything is saved on the phone and sent the moment the network is back. Officers see exactly what is still waiting, and nothing is lost if the server is down for maintenance.

📊

The portfolio at a glance

Outstanding, overdue, PAR 30, DPD ageing and branch performance, live. Import loans from Excel, CSV or JSON, assign them in bulk and export any list.

🛡

Nothing quietly disappears

Every sign-in, user change, import, reassignment and deposit decision is written to an audit log with a name and a time. Companies are kept completely separate.

How it starts

Collecting with LoanDesk within a week.

No implementation project. Three steps, and the first one is ours.

1

We set up your company

You get a company code and an admin login for head office. Choose the plan that fits the number of field officers you have.

2

Import loans, add your team

Upload your loan system’s export or our Excel template, add officers and supervisors with their branch and PIN, and assign loans in bulk.

3

Officers start collecting

They install the app from the browser and sign in. The visit plan is ready, receipts work from the first payment, and you watch it all on the dashboard.
